Metric definitions

MRR / ARR
Sum of contract-line MRR active on the last day of the month; ARR = MRR × 12.
Net revenue retention (NRR)
MRR today from customers who were paying 12 months ago ÷ their MRR 12 months ago. Includes expansion and contraction; churned customers count as $0.
Gross revenue retention (GRR)
Same as NRR, but each customer is capped at their MRR 12 months ago, so expansion can't hide churn.
MRR movements
New (first paid month), reactivation (paid again after $0), expansion, contraction, churn (to $0). Starting MRR + movements = ending MRR; a dbt test enforces this every month.
Cohort revenue retention
Cohort's MRR N months after its first paid month ÷ its starting MRR.
